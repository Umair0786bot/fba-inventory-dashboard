Discount decision — what do we recover, and what surcharge do we escape?

Which lever should we pull?

every SKU priced at its own percentage
Option ? Avg % off ? Avg price ? Cash collected ? Surcharge paid ? Discount given ? Net recovery ? vs no discount ?
Best % is found per SKU by testing every discount from 0% to 90% and keeping the one that leaves the most cash after the surcharge. It is not capped at the ceiling — it often lands lower, because once a smaller cut already clears the stock, going deeper only gives away margin.

Per-SKU detail

Every column header explains itself on hover. Ceiling = the deepest discount that still returns 100% of product cost. Floor = the point where your payout is exactly zero; past it you pay Amazon out of pocket to move the unit. Payout/unit = deal price − Amazon referral − FBA fee (product cost is already spent, so it is not deducted twice). Target SV = the monthly pace needed to clear this SKU's aged stock within a year. To clear figures run the surcharge all the way until the stock is gone — the honest number for SKUs that need more than a year, and the reason holding usually loses.

📈 Units sold per day

Daily units shipped over the tracking window (from Summary USA). The dashed line marks the target daily pace needed to clear the remaining aged units within the coverage window. Bars above the line = on track; below = falling behind.
Target monitoring — SV & DOH
Current SV = units sold ÷ days elapsed (actual pace). Current DOH = remaining units ÷ current SV (days to clear at today's pace). Target SV = remaining ÷ coverage days (pace required). Target DOH = coverage window. Uplift needed = Target SV − Current SV, shown absolute (units/day) and % over current. 🔴 behind = current pace won't clear within the coverage window.

📦 LTSF Command Center --:--
One screen, no clicks — the numbers the other tabs compute, sized to be read from across the room. Sold / cleared / pace come from the tracking window in the workbook; surcharge figures are Amazon's own next-cycle estimate; the cost statement is the 12-month do-nothing total (aged-inventory surcharge + base storage) against the cost of removing the same units to 3PL (removal + freight + 3PL storage). Target pace follows the basis you set on the Sales Tracking tab, so both tabs always agree.
