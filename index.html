Cost source: if the deals file has no Cost/FBA columns, click COGS / Overview tabs and select your per-tag Overview tabs (they carry Cost, FBA and Selling Price) — pick several at once; they merge. Cost priority: tracker → COGS/Overview file; FBA priority: Fee Preview → tracker → COGS/Overview → Default FBA; base price: tracker → COGS/Overview → inventory selling price.
Margin/unit = price − COGS − FBA − (referral% × price). Verdict (one-month rule): a SKU is ✅ RUN when deal margin/unit + AIS/unit ≥ 0 — i.e. selling now (even at a loss) beats paying even one more month of surcharge. Otherwise ⛔ SKIP. Only units aged 181+ drive the impact.
AIS $/unit = SKU's estimated surcharge next month ÷ units being charged. Net/unit = deal margin/unit + AIS/unit (the decision metric; ≥ 0 ⇒ run). Discount cost/unit = base margin − deal margin (margin given up vs selling at base, net of saved referral). The surcharge recurs and escalates monthly, so a positive net/unit understates the real benefit of clearing now.
1-year AIS roll-forward — discount vs no-discount
Closed-cohort roll-forward of today's aged units: each month every unit ages one 30-day band, the oldest band sells off first at the SKU's velocity, and the 181+ bands are charged the aged-inventory surcharge. No new stock is added — this is "what happens to the inventory we hold now."
No-discount sells at current price & velocity (units-shipped-t30). Discount applies a per-SKU %off computed from your loaded deal prices (Deal/COGS uploads on the Deal tab) — base − deal ÷ base; SKUs with no deal price use the fallback % above — and adjusts velocity by the selected amount, clearing faster so fewer months of AIS accrue.
Net = clear-units × margin/unit − projected AIS over the horizon. Action = Discount when discount Net beats no-discount Net. Margin/unit = price − COGS − FBA − referral×price. AIS rate per band is taken from the report's own estimate (estimated-ais ÷ qty-charged); empty bands use a median $/cu-ft card derived across all SKUs × this SKU's volume.
Mo→clear = months until the cohort fully sells through at that velocity. 1yr AIS = surcharge accrued over the horizon. Slow movers that never clear keep accruing — that's where discounting pays off most. Figures use Amazon's own per-band AIS estimates from the inventory report.
